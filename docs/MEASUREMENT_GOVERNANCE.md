# BIS Measurement & Market Positioning Governance

Status: Canonical release constraint

## 1. Current evidence truth

BIS currently has a strong behavioural evidence architecture, especially the BEI/TEI layer. Habit Lab uses BEI-01 through BEI-10 across pre-awareness, baseline profile, prediction calibration, equation confidence, risk, experiment adherence, post shift, post confidence, identity shift and Behaviour Profile Summary.

These are useful behavioural evidence indicators. They are not yet validated psychometric measures.

Current evidence may include:
- self-report scales;
- single 1–10 responses;
- completion and adherence counts;
- reflection outputs;
- facilitator observations;
- descriptive pre/post shifts;
- evidence coverage and confidence metadata.

External and production-facing language must therefore describe BIS outputs as descriptive programme evidence, behavioural evidence indicators, evidence coverage, adherence, observed behaviour or descriptive shifts.

Do not describe current BEI/TEI outputs as psychometrically validated, diagnostic, causal impact measures, validated traits or established predictive measures.

The phrase “forensic marker of behavioural change” is not approved for external measurement claims at the current maturity stage.

## 2. Measurement maturity gate

Higher-order measurement claims remain inactive until the BIS Measurement Manual and validation programme are complete.

The required Measurement Manual must govern at minimum:
- operational definitions;
- scoring rules;
- reverse-scored items where applicable;
- composite formulas;
- missing-data rules;
- observation anchors;
- facilitator/coder calibration;
- aggregation rules;
- evidence-quality weighting;
- benchmark construction;
- inter-rater reliability procedures;
- validation procedures;
- interpretation boundaries.

Until that governance exists, two independent operators must not be allowed to infer different “Behaviour Intelligence” conclusions from the same evidence without an explicit, controlled scoring protocol.

## 3. Operating Architecture status

The Unified Operating Architecture contains valuable future-state structures including the Behaviour Matrix, Evidence Dictionary, Coding Manual, Observation Protocol, Report Engine, QA/certification, data standards and commercial architecture.

These are treated as an implementation and governance roadmap, not as proof that psychometric validation, benchmark validation, predictive validity or causal impact validation have already occurred.

BII scores, behavioural profile classifications, predictive analytics, validated benchmarks and related higher-order outputs must remain feature-gated until the required measurement governance and validation evidence support them.

## 4. Audience architecture

BIS is one content engine serving multiple contexts. Do not create separate curricula where contextual delivery skins are sufficient.

Approved delivery skins:
- School Edition — learner / teacher or facilitator / principal;
- Youth Programme Edition — participant / facilitator / programme manager;
- Workplace Edition — participant / facilitator / L&D or HR.

The Lab intellectual core, evidence model and intervention sequence remain constant. Contextual language, examples, reporting labels and institutional roles may change by skin.

## 5. Commercial product families

Initial commercial packaging should prioritise three existing content families rather than presenting all 32 Labs as an undifferentiated catalogue.

### Personal Operating System
Habit, Decision, Identity, Attention, Time, Risk, Future Self, Resilience.

### Work & Leadership Readiness
Communication, Team, Trust, Influence, Leadership, Negotiation, Ethics, Grit, Personal Effectiveness, Transferable Skills, Launch.

### Applied Commerce & Enterprise Thinking
Money, Entrepreneurship, Opportunity, Customer Thinking, Innovation Thinking, Economics for Humans, Asset Thinking, Financial Philosophy, Systems Thinking, Long-Term Thinking.

Purpose, Growth Mindset and Meta-Learning are cross-cutting Labs and need not become separate initial commercial programmes.

## 6. Release rule

Every future BIS milestone must pass this test before merge or deployment:

1. Does the feature report only evidence the current measurement architecture can defend?
2. Does it avoid implying psychometric validation, diagnosis, causality or predictive validity that has not been established?
3. Does it preserve private learner writing outside institutional reporting?
4. Does it use the correct audience skin without rewriting the intellectual core?
5. Does it strengthen one of the approved commercial families or the shared BIS system?

If any answer is no, the feature must be revised before release.
